1. Nature of Products Offered

Wisper Lab distributes two distinct classes of commercial goods:

  • Digital Goods: Software license keys, downloadable Android APKs/utilities, and electronic publications (PDF, ePub).
  • Physical Goods: Printed books, educational literature, and physical computing kits distributed via domestic postal or courier services.

2. Delivery Timelines and Fulfillment Protocols

  • Digital Product Delivery: Digital licenses and publications are fulfilled electronically. Access links and license keys are dispatched to the customer's provided email address immediately upon payment confirmation (within 0 to 2 hours, and guaranteed within 24 hours under rare technical maintenance).
  • Physical Product Delivery: Physical orders are packaged at our registered dispatch facility in Tanore, Rajshahi, and routed through verified nationwide domestic couriers (e.g. Steadfast, Pathao, or Bangladesh Post Office). Orders within Rajshahi Division are typically delivered within 2–3 business days; orders to all other 63 districts of Bangladesh are delivered within 3–5 business days. Customers receive an SMS/Email consignment tracking code upon dispatch.

3. Refund Eligibility Criteria

We are committed to customer satisfaction and fair trade. You may request a refund or replacement under the following conditions:

  • Defective Digital Products: If a digital download file is corrupted, non-functional, or if a license key fails activation and our support team is unable to rectify the issue within 48 hours of notification.
  • Damaged or Incorrect Physical Items: If a physical book or package arrives damaged in transit, with missing pages, or if an incorrect title was dispatched. You must report the issue within 7 calendar days of parcel receipt with photographic evidence.
  • Non-Delivery: If a physical parcel fails delivery within 10 business days of dispatch due to courier loss, or if an order is cancelled prior to shipment.

4. Non-Refundable Items

Refunds will not be issued in cases where: (a) a digital license key has already been activated and utilized without defect; (b) physical goods have been subjected to intentional damage or altered after delivery; or (c) request is submitted after the 7-day post-delivery notification window.

5. Refund Processing Method and Timeline

Approved refunds are processed through the original payment channel used during purchase:

  • Mobile Financial Services (bKash / Nagad / Rocket): Credited back to the originating mobile account within 3 to 5 business days following refund approval.
  • Bank Transfers & Cards: Credited within 5 to 10 business days depending on your issuing bank's settlement cycle.
